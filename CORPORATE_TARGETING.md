## Corporate — 20-role calibration (2026-08-17)

First full review of the Corporate shortlist (20 roles): **2 Interested / 5 Maybe / 12 Pass / 1 left unrated-but-negative**. A deliberately Pass-heavy batch — very informative about what corporate sourcing over-pulls.

**Positive (thin — only 2 Interested).** Energy / markets analyst work with **modelling** (Shell Energy Analyst — "why not, more soft-skills modelling than only-quant"), and **treasury** (tagged as a good-fit treasury role). Corporate produced few strong fits: most retained roles turned out to be data/IT, tax, accounting, too-junior or too-senior.

**Cautions (Maybe — right area, real reservation).** Treasury but too **narrow** (Holcim guarantees-only — "treasury good fit but looks only guarantees, narrow"); deal finance / M&A but **too senior** (AstraZeneca Manager); product control / commodity trading ("trading not 100% fit"); finance transformation when **too senior**; quant risk when **too code/simulation-heavy** (Statkraft — "too advanced quant, too much in code").

**Clear negatives (Pass — the dominant signal).** **Pure IT / data / analytics-engineering** is the strongest repeated reject: Data Transformation BA, Enterprise Analytics DPM, Controlling Data Science, Network Analytics Engineer, Trading-Systems Developer — all "too data and IT". Then: **tax** ("taxes no"), **accounting**, **junior / internships / working-student** ("too junior"), **HR**, **Director / Partner / Senior-Director** ("too senior"), and pure **project management** ("not into project management").

**Key calibration insight.** The generic "finance transformation / analytics" positive was mis-firing for Corporate: he rejects *pure* data/IT/analytics-engineering even when badged "analytics". Analytics is only attractive as a tool embedded in a finance/investment problem, never as the whole job. Encoded a **pure IT / data-engineering caution** so those roles drop in review order (reorder only, never hard-exclude). Corporate sourcing clearly over-pulls IT/data/tax/accounting roles — worth a future source-side filter, not just review-order.
