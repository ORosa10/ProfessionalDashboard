# Consulting Sector Hypothesis v0.1

Updated: 2026-08-12  
Evidence base: first diverse Big Four calibration cohort, 50/50 reviewed roles

This hypothesis applies primarily to **Big Four and adjacent consulting/advisory employers**. It informs the general profile where signals repeat, but it must not be copied mechanically to Private Markets, Banking, Corporates or other sectors.

## Calibration result

- **14 Interested / 10 Maybe / 26 Pass.** Every role has a written comment.
- Existing automated theme labels are only navigation aids. Several titles were misleading, so sourcing must use the actual role description and experience requirements.

## Consulting lanes to prioritize

1. **Transactions / M&A / Deals**
   - 6 Interested from 8 examples.
   - Prioritize valuation, restructuring, commercial due diligence, transaction services, corporate finance and deal analytics.

2. **Treasury / financial risk / markets**
   - 3 Interested + 1 Maybe from 5 examples.
   - Corporate treasury is a particularly strong direct-experience match.
   - Prefer financial risk, liquidity, funding, hedging and markets work over non-financial risk or highly technical model-development roles.

3. **FP&A / controlling / performance management**
   - All 5 examples remained possible: 2 Interested + 3 Maybe.
   - Prioritize planning, business partnering, performance analysis and decision support.
   - Downrank versions dominated by bookkeeping, closing or statutory reporting.

4. **Finance-linked strategy and transformation**
   - Due diligence, restructuring, financial-services strategy and CFO advisory remain viable.
   - Finance transformation is attractive when the work is led by finance questions.
   - Generic business consulting without meaningful finance content is weaker.

5. **Finance-linked data and analytics**
   - Data is useful when applied to deals, FP&A, treasury, investments or financial risk.
   - Pure data architecture, cloud, cybersecurity and analytics without meaningful finance content are poor fits.

## Consulting lanes to downrank

- Tax and transfer pricing.
- Audit and regulatory assurance.
- Compliance and non-financial risk.
- Actuarial specialisms.
- Pure accounting and statutory reporting.
- SAP, Oracle, D365 or other ERP implementation where platform expertise is the real core of the role.
- Pure IT, cloud, cybersecurity and data architecture.
- Roles whose description contains little substantive finance despite a finance-adjacent title.

These are downranking rules, not permanent deletion rules. Exploration remains available when a role combines a weaker lane with unusually relevant responsibilities.

## Consulting seniority interpretation

- Senior Consultant is the clearest current target.
- Consultant can remain viable when the responsibilities are not genuinely junior.
- Manager roles stay as reach candidates when the content fits; several were attractive but appeared somewhat senior.
- Junior, Graduate, Senior Manager, Director and equivalent extremes receive a strong caution.

## What the next consulting search should emphasize

- Valuation, M&A, Deals, Transaction Services, Restructuring and Commercial Due Diligence.
- Corporate Treasury, liquidity, hedging, capital management and financial/market risk.
- Analytical FP&A, controlling and performance management.
- CFO advisory and finance transformation without heavy ERP dependency.
- Deal analytics and finance-linked data roles.
- Selective financial-services strategy where the work is analytical rather than sales-led or purely regulatory.


## Consulting expansion — 20-role calibration (2026-08-17)

First full review of the Consulting-expansion shortlist (20 roles across Oliver Wyman, Alvarez & Marsal, Horváth, AlixPartners, FTI, Roland Berger, zeb, Capco, L.E.K.): **11 Interested / 6 Maybe / 3 Pass**.

Preferred level is **Senior Consultant / (Senior) Associate** — he positions himself at the senior end and repeatedly noted the roles likely need senior experience.

**Cautions (Maybe — right area, real reservation).** VP+ seniority (too senior); regulation-heavy work (bank steering — "not much experience with regulation"); **real estate** asset management ("if not real estate then fine"); unclear or non-finance sector focus (e.g. "Mobility"); and generic transformation not clearly matching his finance experience. Also flagged: roles badged "Finance & Risk" that may not actually draw on treasury, and practices that aren't really opportunity/investment *evaluation*.

**Clear negatives (Pass).** Operational / organisational-performance work ("too operational"); **wealth management / private banking** ("not into private banking and wealth management"); and **technical / IT / data-&-AI** roles ("too technical and IT").

**Strong-fit consulting firms for him.** Horváth (treasury / CFO / FP&A / working capital) and Alvarez & Marsal (corporate finance / modelling / PE performance) are the clearest matches; FTI and AlixPartners (restructuring, M&A, capital advisory) also land well.
