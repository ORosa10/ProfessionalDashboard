# General Company Targeting Principles (cross-sector)

Scope: cross-sector rules for which *companies* are worth adding to the
Company Universe, independent of any single sector's hypothesis
(`COMPANY_TARGETING.md`). Each sector still keeps its own hypothesis based on
your ratings/notes in that sector; a pattern belongs here only once it
repeats across multiple sectors or reflects a standing preference regardless
of sector (e.g. geography, company size, ownership type).
