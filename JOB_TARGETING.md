# Job Targeting Hypothesis v0.1

Updated: 2026-08-12  
Evidence base: first diverse Big Four calibration cohort, 50/50 reviewed roles

## What the feedback currently says

- **14 Interested / 10 Maybe / 26 Pass.** Every role has a written comment.
- **Transactions / M&A:** 6 Interested from 8 examples. Prioritize valuation, restructuring, deals, commercial due diligence, corporate finance and transaction-oriented roles.
- **Treasury / financial risk / markets:** 3 Interested + 1 Maybe from 5 examples. Corporate treasury is a particularly strong direct-experience match. Prefer financial risk and markets; downrank non-financial risk and highly technical quant/model-development roles.
- **FP&A / controlling:** all 5 examples remained possible (2 Interested + 3 Maybe), but accounting-heavy versions were repeatedly qualified. Prioritize planning, business partnering, performance analysis and decision support over bookkeeping/reporting ownership.
- **Strategy / consulting:** mixed but viable (3 Maybe from 5, plus transaction-strategy roles rated Interested). Prefer due diligence, financial-services strategy, restructuring and finance-linked strategy over generic business consulting.
- **Finance transformation:** attractive only when led by CFO/finance questions. Downrank roles whose real core is SAP, Oracle, D365, cloud architecture or another vendor implementation.
- **Data / analytics:** useful as an enabling skill inside finance, investments, deals, risk or FP&A. Pure data architecture, IT transformation, cloud, cybersecurity and analytics without meaningful finance content should be downranked.
- **Clear negative signals:** tax, audit, regulatory/non-financial compliance, actuarial specialisms, pure accounting, pure IT and roles without meaningful finance content.

## Seniority hypothesis

- Primary band: **Senior Analyst / Analyst where investment-track titles run lower / Associate / Senior Consultant / Specialist** with roughly 3–7 years as a directional guide.
- **Manager is not a hard rejection.** Several Manager roles were attractive on content but felt somewhat senior. Retain them as reach candidates when responsibilities plausibly fit.
- Penalize true graduate, internship, assistant and clearly junior roles.
- Strongly downrank Senior Manager, Director, Head and equivalent leadership roles unless the stated experience requirement is unexpectedly accessible.
- Decide from responsibilities and experience requirements, not title alone; PE and investment-banking title ladders differ from consulting and corporate finance.

## Constraints kept separate from intrinsic role interest

- German is approximately B2: roles requiring business-fluent, native or C1/C2 German need a visible language caution, not automatic deletion.
- Pure client acquisition or sales-led roles remain a lower priority, while advisory interaction itself is acceptable.
- Company interest, contacts, compensation viability and location economics must remain separate signals. They can affect ordering later but must not rewrite the intrinsic role feedback.

## Search targeting for the next sourcing runs

Prioritize:

1. M&A, valuation, transaction services, restructuring, commercial due diligence and corporate finance.
2. Corporate treasury, liquidity, funding, hedging, market/financial risk and capital management.
3. FP&A, controlling, performance management and finance business partnering with analytical decision support.
4. Investment, portfolio, asset-management and private-markets roles using modelling, due diligence or portfolio analysis.
5. CFO advisory and finance transformation when the work is finance-led rather than ERP-led.
6. Finance-linked analytics and data roles where data is a tool rather than the profession's main subject.

Downrank, but do not permanently delete:

- tax, audit, compliance, non-financial risk and actuarial roles;
- pure accounting/reporting roles;
- ERP/vendor implementation and pure IT/data architecture;
- clearly junior or clearly senior-leadership roles;
- roles whose language or technical requirements create a material feasibility gap.

## Exploration rule

This is an early calibration based on a deliberately varied sample, not a final scoring model. Keep roughly **20% exploration capacity** for adjacent or uncertain roles. A low initial fit must lower review priority, not cause hard exclusion, so the system can still discover unexpected good matches.
